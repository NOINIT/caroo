About Clanoo

What is Clanoo?

A family app in which grandma or grandpa takes part too. She has her own simple screen on her tablet or phone. The family uses Clanoo on their own phones. So you can see how she is doing, who is coming to visit and what is needed.

When can I start using Clanoo?

Clanoo is coming in 2027. Put yourself on the waiting list and you will be the first to hear.

Who is Clanoo for?

For families who care for an older relative together, close by or far away. And for older people who want to stay in touch with their family and a few friends themselves. You can also start the group for yourself.

Price

What does Clanoo cost?

The first month is free, without payment details. After that Clanoo costs €12.50 a month or €125 a year. That is per family, including VAT.

Who pays?

Whoever starts the group. Everyone who joins takes part for free, with as many family members as you like.

What is the founder price?

The first 100 families who take out a subscription pay €69 for the first year instead of €125. After that it is €125 a year.

Can I subscribe during the free month?

Yes. You only pay once the free month is over.

How do I cancel?

A monthly subscription can be cancelled every month. A yearly subscription can be cancelled at any time, and you keep Clanoo until the end of that year. After cancelling you can still download the stories for 30 days.

For grandma or grandpa

Does grandma need to know how to use a computer?

No. Her screen has large buttons and clear letters. Fancy a visit? One tap on I'd like a visit. How is today going? One smiley. The family sets Clanoo up for her.

Does she need a password?

No. The family links her tablet or phone once with a QR code. After that Clanoo simply stays open. She does not need an email address either.

What device do you need?

A tablet or phone with internet, via wifi or a SIM card. That can be an iPad or iPhone, or an Android tablet or phone. You download Clanoo from the App Store or Google Play. Many older people find a tablet easier, because everything is bigger.

Does grandma have a say?

Yes. How she feels and which medicines she takes is only in Clanoo if she gives permission. She can withdraw that permission at any time. She chooses her own friends in the app.

Can she keep in touch with friends?

Yes, with up to three friends she already knows who also use Clanoo. A friendship only starts once both of them say yes. They can send each other messages and photos and play games together, such as draughts and chess. The family does not read along.

Safety and privacy

Can a stranger get in?

No. Only people the group's admin invites can join. Every invitation is personal and valid for 7 days, and joining is only possible with your own account. So it stays a trusted circle, without scam messages from strangers. There are no ads either.

Where is our data stored?

In Europe, in Ireland, where the GDPR applies. We do not sell data and we show no ads. Everything is in the privacy statement (in Dutch).

Is Clanoo an alarm button or a medical app?

No. In an emergency call 112, and for medical questions go to the GP. The medicines in Clanoo are a reminder for the family.

Family

Does Clanoo work if the family lives far away?

Yes. Clanoo works wherever there is internet, also outside the Netherlands. Whether you live around the corner or in Paramaribo.

Which languages does Clanoo work in?

Dutch, English and Turkish. Everyone chooses their own language. If grandma asks for a visit in Turkish, her grandson gets the notification in Dutch.
