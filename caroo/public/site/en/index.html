For the family

All the care in one place. Nobody has to remember everything alone.

In touch, never with strangers.

Fake messages, false bank emails, someone pretending to be a grandchild: grandparents get more and more of them. In Clanoo, nobody can just walk in. Only family and friends they really know. That way it stays their own, trusted circle.
